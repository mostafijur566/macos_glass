## 0.1.0

First release.

- `GlassApp`, `GlassWindowConfig`, `GlassWindow` — full-size content view with a unified title bar and native sidebar vibrancy.
- `GlassSidebar` with search filtering and System Settings–style coloured icon tiles (`GlassIconTile`).
- `GlassPage` — back button and title in the title bar, glass toolbar actions (`GlassTitlebarPassthrough`).
- Forms: `GlassFormSection`, `GlassFormRow`, `GlassGroup`.
- Controls: `GlassButton` (primary, bezel, glass, destructive, plain; small/regular/large), `GlassIconButton`, `GlassTextField`, `GlassPopupButton`, `GlassSegmentedControl`, `GlassDateField` / `GlassCalendar`.
- `GlassSwitch` matched to macOS 26/27 System Settings: 36 × 16 pt track, 21 × 14 pt pill knob, stretch-on-press; `mini` and `large` sizes. `GlassInfoButton`.
- Overlays: `showGlassMenu`, `showGlassAlert`, `showGlassSheet`, `GlassTooltip`.
- `GlassSurface` (blur + tint + sheen + shadow), `GlassPressable`, `GlassTheme` / `GlassThemeData` (light & dark, custom accent).
